The first morning in Manali I woke up at 5am because the cold had quietly slid under the door. I made tea on the small electric coil in the room and stood on the balcony in two sweaters, watching the deodar pines emerge out of the dark like they were apologizing for something.

I had come without a plan, which is rare for me. Usually I arrive somewhere with a list, a map, three saved restaurants. But Himachal asked for nothing and I gave it nothing back. I read for hours. I walked the same lane to the same tea shop every afternoon. The owner stopped asking what I wanted and just started pouring.

> *Some places are meant to be ticked off, others are meant to dissolve a little of the speed you arrived with.*

A monkey stole half an apple from me on the third day. I laughed for a long time afterward, in the way you can only laugh alone, walking, the air thin and clean.

If you go: take warm clothes even in May. Take a notebook. Don't take a tight schedule.
